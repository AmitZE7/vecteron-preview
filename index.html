From reservation to first rack

  1. First, you reserve

    Tell us the load, density and target date. We hold that capacity for you while terms are agreed.

  2. Then a design workshop

    Your engineers and ours fix the rack layout, the cooling mode for each row and the power per rack.

  3. Contract and rate lock

    Founding customers fix their rate for the initial term before the first hall is energised.

  4. Build and fit-out

    Your suite is built to the agreed design. You visit the site at each milestone.

  5. Testing under full load

    Every system runs under simulated full load before handover, with integrated commissioning at level 5.

  6. Your first rack goes live

    Remote hands, a monitoring portal and 24/7 operations from the first day.

Questions we hear first

Where: Modimolle, Limpopo, off the N1. We share the exact site under NDA.

Status: pre-leasing. Reserved customers get the build programme.

Visits: site walks by appointment.

Why Modimolle and not Johannesburg?

Land and power connection capacity are easier to secure here than in the metro, and the distance makes Leadwood a true second site for Gauteng: a different municipal grid, a separate fibre route and no shared flood risk, but still about two hours away by road.

Is it a certified Tier III facility?

It is designed to Tier III: every component can be maintained without shutting down your load. We will apply to the Uptime Institute for certification of the design documents and then of the constructed facility. Until those certificates are issued, we call it Tier III-design.

What happens during load-shedding?

The campus moves to battery storage and on-site solar, with diesel generators carrying the full load behind them. Fuel stored on site covers 72 hours at full load, and fuel supply contracts cover longer events.

Can you take liquid-cooled GPU racks?

Yes. That is what the halls are designed for. Direct-to-chip cooling supports up to 130 kW per rack, and rear-door heat exchangers support up to 50 kW. Tell us your platform and we will design the row around it.

Does the cooling use a lot of water?

No. Liquid cooling here runs in a closed loop, and heat is rejected through dry coolers rather than evaporative towers, so the campus uses almost no water for cooling. That matters in a water-scarce province.

What does a reservation commit me to?

A reservation holds capacity while we agree terms. Nothing is binding until both sides sign a colocation agreement.
