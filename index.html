Ask a question
Why Modimolle rather than Johannesburg?

Land and grid connection capacity are easier to secure outside the metro, and the distance makes MDM1 a genuine second site for Gauteng: a different municipal grid, a separate fibre route and no shared flood or metro risk, while still being about two hours away by road.

Is MDM1 a certified Tier III facility?

MDM1 is designed to Tier III, so every component can be maintained without taking customer load down. We will apply to the Uptime Institute for certification of the design documents and then of the constructed facility. Until those certificates are issued, we describe the campus as Tier III-design.

What happens during load-shedding?

The campus rides through on battery storage and on-site solar, with N+1 diesel generation carrying the full load behind them. Fuel stored on site covers 72 hours at full load, and refuelling contracts cover longer events. Customers see no interruption.

Can you host liquid-cooled GPU racks?

Yes. That is what the halls are designed for. Direct-to-chip cooling supports up to 130 kW per rack and rear-door heat exchangers support up to 50 kW. Tell us your platform and we design the row around it.

How much water does the cooling use?

Almost none. Liquid cooling runs in closed loops and heat is rejected through dry coolers rather than evaporative towers. That matters in a water-scarce province and it keeps operating costs predictable.

What does a reservation commit me to?

A reservation holds capacity while we agree terms and gives you a seat in the design workshops. Nothing is binding until both parties sign a colocation agreement.

Can I visit the site?

Yes. Site walks are arranged by appointment for qualified customers, and the exact location is shared under NDA.
